\part{Proposal}
\label{part:Proposal}

\emph{This proposal sets out the objectives, approach and plan of work for
the assigned project over the two semesters.}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:Introduction}

Introduce the project: its background, context and the problem it addresses.
Example citation \autocite{lamport_time_1978}.

\paragraph{Motivation}
Explain why the project is worth doing: the gap or need it responds to, and
who benefits from its outcome.

\section{Objectives and Scope}
\label{sec:Objectives}

State what the project sets out to achieve and the boundaries within which
it will be carried out.

\paragraph{Objectives}
List the concrete objectives of the project and how their achievement will
be judged.

\paragraph{Scope}
Delimit what is in and out of scope, and state any assumptions and
constraints.

\paragraph{Requirements}\label{sec:Requirements}
Capture the key requirements as user stories (move to an appendix if too
long).

\section{Approach}
\label{sec:Approach}

Describe how the group intends to approach the project topic: the
methodology, the technologies or techniques being considered, and the
rationale for these choices.

\paragraph{Design Decisions}
Record the key design decisions, the alternatives considered, and the
reasons for each choice.

\paragraph{Project Plan}
Present the plan of work over the two semesters, aligned to the submission
deadlines (Table~\ref{tab:Timeline}).
