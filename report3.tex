\part{Final Report}
\label{part:Final}

\emph{This final report presents the completed project, its evaluation, and
the application of knowledge acquired through the programme.}

\section{Implementation}
\label{sec:Implementation}

Describe what was ultimately built and delivered: the final state of the
project and how it fulfils the objectives in Part~\ref{part:Proposal}.

\paragraph{Design Overview}
Present the final design or architecture, highlighting the key decisions and
their trade-offs.

\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:Evaluation}

Explain how the project outcome was evaluated: the methodology, setup and
metrics used.

\paragraph{Results}
Present and interpret the results, relating them back to the objectives and
the user stories (Section~\ref{sec:Requirements}).

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:Conclusion}

\paragraph{Summary}
Summarize what was achieved, measured against the objectives set out in
Part~\ref{part:Proposal}.

% This reflection is the core of the course objective: how concepts and
% theories gained in the classroom translate into (industrial) practice.
\paragraph{Reflection}\label{sec:Reflection}
Reflect on how the concepts and theories from your degree programme were
applied in the project, and how they shaped its outcome.

\paragraph{Future Work}
Suggest extensions or improvements that could follow from this project.
